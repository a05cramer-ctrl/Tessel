How it works

One flow. Every coin.

01

Choose the basket

Pick a theme: AI & agents, dogs, cats, frogs, the ten biggest coins. Each lists every coin it holds and why it is in.

02

Enter your SOL amount

One number. Tessel splits it by the basket's weights, capped at 20% per coin, and shows you the exact coins you will receive.

03

One transaction

The coins land in your own wallet, not in a pooled token. Sell any of them whenever you like.
